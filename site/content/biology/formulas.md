# Biology — Key Equations <span class="zh">重要方程式</span>

## Respiration <span class="zh">呼吸作用</span>

**{{Aerobic respiration|有氧呼吸}}** (in mitochondria):

$$\text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6 + 6\,\text{O}_2 \longrightarrow 6\,\text{CO}_2 + 6\,\text{H}_2\text{O} + \text{energy (ATP)}$$

**{{Anaerobic respiration|无氧呼吸}}**:

| Organism | Equation |
|---|---|
| Animals (muscles) | $\text{glucose} \longrightarrow \text{lactic acid} + \text{a little energy}$ |
| Yeast and plants | $\text{glucose} \longrightarrow \text{ethanol} + \text{carbon dioxide} + \text{a little energy}$ |

## Photosynthesis <span class="zh">光合作用</span>

$$6\,\text{CO}_2 + 6\,\text{H}_2\text{O} \xrightarrow[\text{chlorophyll}]{\text{light}} \text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6 + 6\,\text{O}_2$$

## Microscopy <span class="zh">显微镜</span>

$$\text{Magnification} = \frac{\text{image size}}{\text{actual size}}$$

| Unit | Equals |
|---|---|
| 1 mm | 1000 µm |
| 1 µm | 1000 nm |

## Surface area to volume ratio

$$\text{SA : V ratio} = \frac{\text{surface area}}{\text{volume}}$$

A smaller cell has a **larger** SA : V ratio, so diffusion supplies it more efficiently.

## Enzyme summary

| Factor | Effect on rate |
|---|---|
| Temperature ↑ (below optimum) | ↑ more collisions |
| Temperature ↑ (above optimum) | ↓ denaturation |
| pH away from optimum | ↓ active site shape changes |
| Substrate concentration ↑ | ↑ then levels off |
| Enzyme concentration ↑ | ↑ (if substrate is in excess) |
